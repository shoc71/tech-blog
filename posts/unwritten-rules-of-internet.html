With the old adage The Internet never forgets, we need to remember how we conduct ourselves online because we don't know when our old actions and words will come back to bite us back in horrific ways, which will never be forgotten. If, for a second, you believe that you have any semblence of online privacy, I hate to be the one that bursts your bubble but you should always assume

  • 1. You're constantly being monitored by people you'll never meet
  • 2. Your data is constantly being collected
  • 3. Your data was bought and sold a long time ago

Without getting too off point, my intention is not to scare you off the internet but to remind you to protect yourself from the normies online that try to stalk you. It's meaningless to stop (some* of) them but I would encourage you to make it as difficult as you can possibly can. Make it so painful that they will want to give up from trying a second time. Anyone with either the useful knowledge, enough determination (autistic people: none), or proper credentials can easily access a ton of your online data and peace together things about you that will frankly scare you. In the rare off chance you encounter one that has met any or all of the conditions, know that you have really pis-sed(uncensor dash) them off.

I'd congratulate you but I want nothing to do with the dangerous man you invited to this party.

Side tangent aside, a lot of the laws surrounding tech and our online behavior have not yet caught with our modern usage and actions.
